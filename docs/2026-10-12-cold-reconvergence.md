# Cold-call rendezvous experiment (2026-10-12)

Default OFF: `PEPEPOW_CUDA_COLD_RECONVERGENCE`. It selects a new noinline mixer only for the existing local-phase Header80 kernel. Every member of the function-entry active mask advances its own cells until its next nonzero cold cell or completion, rendezvous precedes nonlinear evaluation, and another rendezvous precedes the next collective completion check. Finished lanes keep participating until the entire entry mask finishes. There are no block-wide collectives or cross-warp state transfers.

Per-lane invariants: rows0..63 and columns0..63 remain ordered; zero cold nibbles skip the contribution but retain their SW update; warm contributions and nonzero-cold FP64 expressions use existing helpers/order; row sums reset only at row boundaries; SW persists across rows; even/odd sums convert independently before integer addition; byte packing and output words retain the original pair order. Input words are cached only across their eight cells, matching the preceding word-stream layout. Final BLAKE3 and target publication are unchanged.

This is an experiment, not a proved consensus optimization. Compiler contraction/code generation and collective participation need actual CUDA validation. Test full/partial warp and block sizes, nonce boundaries, maximum/minimum/below-minimum targets and ANY qualifying nonce selection; repeat maximum after empty result to detect stale state.
